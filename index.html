Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026.

Songs, microphone and practice information

Singing App processes imported audio and microphone input on your device. The app does not upload your songs, singing audio, optional recordings, pitch traces or practice scores to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, tracking, account registration or a third-party analytics SDK.

Microphone permission lets the app compare your singing with a reference during practice. Saving voice audio is optional and starts off. Music Library permission lets you select downloaded, unprotected songs. Files access is granted for files you choose. You can manage permissions in device Settings.

The app stores copies of imported songs, their analyses, practice scores and pitch graphs locally. Optional voice recordings use additional storage. Local timing and playback diagnostic files may contain song names, playback positions and audio-route information for troubleshooting; the app does not automatically send them to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Saved songs and practice information remain until removed through the app or its local storage is removed. You can delete a song, a practice attempt, or just an attempt's recording. Deleting a recording keeps its results. Removing the app can remove its local songs and history. Your original selected audio file is not deleted by the app. Depending on your system settings, app data may be included in device backups; those backups are controlled by your platform provider and account settings.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple's TestFlight service can provide the developer with tester information, usage information, crash diagnostics and feedback. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information: Apple’s TestFlight privacy information . Beta feedback is used to investigate problems and improve this app, not for advertising. Do not include recordings or other personal information in feedback unless you intend to share it. The developer does not sell beta feedback or share it with unrelated third parties.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email singingapp.support@gmail.com or use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Please include your app version and a short description of the problem. Only attach audio or other personal material if you intend to share it. Support messages are handled through Gmail and used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the app. Avoid sending passwords or sensitive personal information.

Changes

The policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. This policy describes the current beta; any later cloud processing, accounts or sharing features require a new review.
